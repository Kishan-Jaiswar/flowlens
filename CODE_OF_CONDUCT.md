# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is trying to build something and
has less context than you do.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique code, not people.
- Explain the reasoning, not just the verdict. "This is wrong" helps nobody;
  "this breaks when the frontend interpolates the path, see the asymmetric
  scoring in ARCHITECTURE.md" helps everybody.
- Assume good faith when a report is vague or a patch is rough. Ask for what you
  need.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to kishan-jaiswar via GitHub. Reports are handled privately.
Anything that violates the above may be edited or removed, and repeated or
severe behaviour may result in being blocked from the project.
